§ 101.010 – Name of Code

This body of law shall be cited as the San Andreas Criminal Code, abbreviated as SACC.

§ 101.020 – Definitions

For the purposes of this Code, 'person' includes individuals, corporations, and entities recognized by law.

§ 102.010 – Categories of Offenses

Criminal offenses are classified from Misdemeanor to Category A Felony based on severity.

§ 102.020 – Scaled Sentencing for RP

Each classification corresponds to in-game time in seconds ranging from 15 to 120, increasing with severity. See the sentencing scale below:

Classification Max RP Time (seconds)
Misdemeanor15
Gross Misdemeanor25
Category E Felony40
Category D Felony55
Category C Felony70
Category B Felony95
Category A Felony120
